Digital Safety Tips

Practical steps to help you, your family and your community stay safer online.

Secure Your Accounts

  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever available.
  • Use a unique, strong password for each account. Consider a password manager.
  • Review and remove devices/sessions you don't recognize in account settings.

Manage Your Privacy Settings

  • Set social media profiles to private and review who can see your posts and location.
  • Turn off location sharing/tagging unless you need it.
  • Regularly review app permissions (camera, mic, contacts, location).

Recognize Phishing & Social Engineering

  • Be cautious of unexpected links, urgent requests, or offers that feel too good to be true.
  • Verify requests for money or personal info through a separate, trusted channel.
  • Never share passwords or one-time codes with anyone.

Protect Against Stalkerware

  • Watch for unfamiliar apps, fast battery drain, or unusual data use.
  • Keep your device's operating system and apps updated.
  • Set a device passcode and avoid sharing it, even with a partner.

Be Deepfake-Aware

  • Be skeptical of shocking images/videos. Check for visual inconsistencies.
  • Know that AI-generated content can look highly realistic; verify before you believe or share.
  • If targeted by a deepfake, save evidence and report it. See Get Help.

Document Safely

  • Screenshot abusive content with dates/usernames visible before it's deleted.
  • Keep records in a place the abuser cannot access.
  • Use each platform's official reporting tools for harassment or non-consensual content.
For Parents & Guardians

Keeping girls safe online

  • Have open, judgment-free conversations about online experiences.
  • Co-set privacy settings on new apps and devices together.
  • Know the reporting tools on the platforms your children use.
  • Teach that no one should ever ask them for private images or personal information.
