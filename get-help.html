Before You Act

How to preserve evidence safely

  • Take screenshots of messages, posts, profiles and URLs, including dates and timestamps where visible.
  • Avoid replying to or engaging with the person harassing you. It can escalate the situation.
  • Do not delete the harmful content yet; save it first, even if it's distressing.
  • Use each platform's built-in "Report" feature to flag abusive content or accounts.
  • Block the person only after you've saved the evidence you need.
  • Adjust your account privacy and security settings. See our Digital Safety Tips.
