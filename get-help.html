Next steps

What you can do right now

You do not have to do everything at once. Take the steps that feel safe for you, in your own time.

Contact Malaq for support

  1. Make sure you are safe

    If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services first. Your physical safety comes before anything else.

  2. Save the evidence

    Screenshot messages, posts and profiles, including usernames, dates and links. Store copies where the person harming you cannot reach them.

  3. Do not engage

    Avoid replying to or confronting the person. Engaging can escalate the abuse or be used against you.

  4. Report and block

    Use the platform's reporting tools for abusive content or accounts, and block the person once you have saved what you need.

  5. Reach out for support

    Talk to a helpline, a trusted person or our team. Support can help you plan what comes next.

Browsing safely

Protect your privacy while you look for help

If someone may check your phone or computer, these steps can reduce the chance they see what you have been reading.

Use Quick exit

The red button, or Esc three times, replaces this page with a neutral site.

Browse privately

A private or incognito window does not save the pages you visit in your history.

Clear your history

Remove individual pages rather than everything, as a fully empty history can raise suspicion.

Use a safer device

A trusted friend's phone or a public computer is harder for someone else to monitor.
