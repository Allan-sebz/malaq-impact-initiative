Overview

This policy explains how Malaq Impact Initiative ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website. This is an informational website: it has no user accounts, no database and no advertising or analytics tracking.

Information we collect

The only personal information we collect is what you choose to send through our contact form:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your organisation, if you provide one
  • The reason for contacting us and your message

We do not collect personal information anywhere else on this site. The digital safety check on our digital safety page runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you tick is stored or sent.

How forms are processed

Our contact form is powered by Formspree, a form-processing service that delivers your message to our inbox. Formspree handles this data on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

We use your information only to respond to your message. We never sell, rent or share it with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver them.

How long we keep data

We keep contact form messages only for as long as we need them to respond to you and follow up on your request, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete any personal information you have sent us. To make a request, please get in touch through our contact page.

Safety features

Every page includes a Quick exit button, and pressing Esc three times does the same. Both replace this site with a neutral page and remove it from the back button, but they cannot erase your device's browsing history. Our browsing safely tips explain how to protect your privacy further.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time, and any changes will be posted on this page. If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.
