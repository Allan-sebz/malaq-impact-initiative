The Problem

Digital spaces are not yet safe and equal spaces

Technology-facilitated gender-based violence is any act of gender-based violence that is committed, assisted, amplified, or enabled through digital technologies, the internet, or electronic devices. It is broader than "online abuse": technology can be the tool used to commit the violence, or it can make existing gender-based violence more severe.

Our Approach

Three Strategic Pillars

01
Protect

Prevent and respond to technology-facilitated GBV, provide survivor-centred support, and improve access to justice.

02
Empower

Equip women and girls with digital-safety knowledge, tools and resources to participate confidently and safely online.

03
Transform

Drive research, policy reform, institutional change and safer technology practices to create a more accountable digital ecosystem.

Know the Signs

What TFGBV can look like

↻

Online Harassment

Threatening or sexually harassing messages on WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram and other platforms.

◆

AI Deepfakes

Fake sexual images or videos created and distributed without consent.

⌖

Digital Stalking & Coercive Control

Tracking someone's location, monitoring their phone, or using spyware to control a partner.
