About Malaq Impact Initiative

Building a Uganda where women and girls can participate in digital life safely, freely and equally.

Our Mission

Why we exist

Our mission is to eliminate technology-facilitated gender-based violence in Uganda by transforming how individuals, communities, institutions, governments and technology companies prevent, detect and respond to digital harm, while empowering women and girls to participate safely, freely and equally in Uganda's digital society.

Our Vision

The future we're working toward

A Uganda where every woman and girl can use technology and digital spaces freely, safely and with dignity, free from online violence, exploitation and coercive control, and supported by institutions and technology companies that are accountable and responsive.

Our Approach

Three Strategic Pillars

01
Protect

Prevent and respond to technology-facilitated GBV, provide survivor-centred support services, and improve survivors' access to justice through partnerships with law enforcement, legal aid and health services.

02
Empower

Equip women and girls with digital-safety knowledge, tools and resources, from account security to recognizing manipulation, so they can participate confidently and safely online.

03
Transform

Drive research, policy reform, institutional change and safer technology practices, working with government and tech platforms to build a more accountable digital ecosystem.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Survivor-Centred

Every response starts with the safety, dignity and choices of the survivor.

Rights-Based

We anchor our work in the right to a safe, free and equal digital life.

Evidence-Driven

We ground our programs and advocacy in research and documented realities.

Collaborative

We work with communities, government, and technology companies, not around them.
