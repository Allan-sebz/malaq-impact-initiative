About Malaq Impact Initiative

A Uganda-based initiative dedicated to addressing technology-facilitated gender-based violence and promoting safer, more inclusive digital environments.

Who we are

Where digital safety meets gender justice

We work at the intersection of digital safety, gender justice, survivor support, research, policy and technology.

We work with individuals, communities, institutions, government and technology companies to prevent, detect and respond to technology-facilitated harm, enabling women and girls to participate in Uganda’s digital society safely, freely and equally.

Our mission

To eliminate technology-facilitated gender-based violence in Uganda by transforming how individuals, communities, institutions, governments and technology companies prevent, detect and respond to digital harm, while empowering women and girls to participate safely, freely and equally in Uganda's digital society.

Our vision

A Uganda where everyone can participate in digital spaces safely, freely and with dignity.

In that future, survivors are believed and supported, perpetrators are held to account, and the institutions and technology companies that shape digital life are responsive to the people they serve.
