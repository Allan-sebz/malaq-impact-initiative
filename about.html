Safer digital lives for women and girls

Malaq Impact Initiative exists to end technology-facilitated gender-based violence in Uganda, and to make digital spaces safe, free and equal for everyone.

Our mission

To eliminate technology-facilitated gender-based violence in Uganda by transforming how individuals, communities, institutions, governments and technology companies prevent, detect and respond to digital harm, while empowering women and girls to participate safely, freely and equally in Uganda's digital society.

Image: the team or a community engagement session

Our vision

A Uganda where every woman and girl is safe online

We picture a Uganda where women and girls use technology and digital spaces freely, safely and with dignity, free from online violence, exploitation and coercive control.

In that future, survivors are believed and supported, perpetrators are held to account, and the institutions and technology companies that shape digital life are responsive to the people they serve.

How we work

Three strategic pillars

Each pillar tackles a different part of the problem. Together they move from immediate support for survivors to long-term change in the systems around them.

01

Protect

Prevent and respond to technology-facilitated GBV, provide survivor-centred support and improve access to justice through partnerships with legal, health and law enforcement services.

02

Empower

Equip women and girls with digital-safety knowledge, tools and resources, from account security to spotting manipulation, so they can participate confidently and safely online.

03

Transform

Drive research, policy reform, institutional change and safer technology practices, working with government and platforms to build a more accountable digital ecosystem.

What guides us

Our values

01

Survivor-centred

Every response starts with the safety, dignity and choices of the survivor.

02

Rights-based

We anchor our work in every person's right to a safe, free and equal digital life.

03

Evidence-driven

Our programmes and advocacy are grounded in research and documented experience.

04

Collaborative

We work with communities, government and technology companies, not around them.
