Every voice counts

Start where you are

You do not need to join an organisation to make a difference. Awareness is one of the strongest tools against technology-facilitated violence.

  • Share reliable information

    Pass on our guides to friends, family and colleagues. Someone in your circle may need them.

  • Start the conversation

    Raise the issue in your school, workplace, place of worship or community group.

  • Learn the signs

    Understand the forms TFGBV takes so you can recognise it and respond with care.

  • Believe survivors

    If someone tells you they are being abused online, listen, believe them and point them to support.
